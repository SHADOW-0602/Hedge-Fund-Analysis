Quick Links: Data & Uploads

1) Portfolio CSV Input

  • Upload your current holdings to initialize your portfolio.
  • Required columns: Symbol, Quantity, Avg_Cost.
  • Headers are case-insensitive (e.g., "avg_cost", "Avg Cost", "Average Cost").
  • Custom columns are preserved by our Smart Enrichment system.
portfolio_import.csv
Symbol * Quantity * Avg_Cost * Sector (Optional)
AAPL 100 $150.50 Technology
MSFT 50 $280.00 Technology
NVDA 25 $450.00 Semiconductors
VOO 10 $400.00 ETF
GOOGL 25 $2500.00 Communication

2) Transaction CSV Input

  • Log your trade history for accurate P&L and XIRR analysis.
  • Required columns: Date, Symbol, Type, Qty, Price.
  • Accepted Types: BUY, SELL, DIVIDEND, SPLIT.
  • Dates should be formatted as YYYY-MM-DD.

Brokerage Support & Plaid

Connecting Your Account

We use Plaid to securely connect to over 12,000 financial institutions.

  1. Click the Connect Account button in the dashboard.
  2. Select your brokerage (e.g., Robinhood, Fidelity, Vanguard).
  3. Authenticate securely through your bank's portal.

We strictly use read-only access. We never store your bank credentials.

Analytics Explained

Smart Enrichment

Our system automatically fills in missing data. If your manual CSV lacks sector info, we fetch it. If your Plaid data lacks cost basis, we infer it from your transaction history.

XIRR vs TWR

XIRR (Money-Weighted Return) accounts for the timing of your deposits. TWR (Time-Weighted Return) isolates your investment skill by ignoring cash flows.

Risk Metrics (VaR)

Value at Risk (VaR) estimates the maximum potential loss over a specific time frame at a given confidence level (e.g., 95%).

Frequently Asked Questions

General & Account

How do you handle privacy?

We prioritize security. Plaid handles all bank credentials securely. We only access read-only transaction data to generate your analytics.

Is there a mobile app?

Our dashboard is fully responsive. You can access it on any smartphone or tablet via your browser for a seamless experience.

Does SHM Ventures offer trading?

No, we are purely an analytics platform. We help you analyze your portfolio, but trades must be executed through your brokerage.

How do I switch between Light and Dark mode?

Use the toggle switch in the top-right corner of the header. Your preference is saved automatically.

Can I export my reports?

Currently, reports are viewed on the dashboard. PDF export functionality is on our roadmap for Q3.

Data & Uploads

Can I upload data and use Plaid together?

Yes! Our Data Merger combines them. Manual uploads generally override Plaid data for the same ticker, giving you full control.

What if my brokerage isn't supported by Plaid?

You can use the Manual CSV Upload feature. See the "Quick Links" section above for the required format.

How do I delete my data?

You can reset your portfolio in the Settings menu (coming soon). For now, uploading an empty CSV file will clear your manual data.

Do I need to clean my CSV file?

We support Regex Mapping, so minor header variations (e.g., "Avg Cost" vs "avg_cost") are handled automatically. However, ensure special characters are removed.

Does Plaid access expire?

Access tokens may expire periodically for security. If your data stops syncing, simply click "Connect Account" again to re-authenticate.

Portfolio Analytics

Why is my XIRR showing 0%?

XIRR requires both positive (Buy) and negative (Sell/Current Value) flows. If you only have Buys without a current valuation, or missing Cost Basis, it may return 0.

What accounting method is used for PnL?

Our Advanced Transaction Analyzer calculates PnL using both FIFO (First-In-First-Out) and LIFO (Last-In-First-Out). You can view both in the detailed report.

How often are stock prices updated?

Prices are fetched in real-time (or 15-min delay depending on the exchange) via our data partners like Polygon.io and Alpha Vantage.

What is Smart Enrichment?

It's our background process that fills gaps. If your data misses "Sector" or "Industry", we fetch it automatically using the ticker symbol.

Why is my Total Return different from my broker's?

Brokers often use "Simple Return". We provide "Time-Weighted Return" (TWR) and XIRR, which are more accurate measures of your actual investment performance over time.

Technical & Risk

What is Value at Risk (VaR)?

VaR estimates the maximum loss you might expect over a given period (e.g., 1 day) with 95% confidence. It helps you quantify downside risk.

Can I customize the charts?

Yes, our Technical Analysis charts powered by TradingView allow you to add indicators like RSI, MACD, and Bollinger Bands.

What is the Sharpe Ratio?

It measures risk-adjusted return. A higher Sharpe Ratio (typically > 1.0) means you are getting better returns for the amount of risk taken.

Do you support Options charts?

We currently visualize payoff diagrams for basic option strategies. Comprehensive Greeks and volatility surfaces are in development.

What does "Beta" mean?

Beta measures volatility relative to the market. Beta > 1 means your portfolio is more volatile than the S&P 500; Beta < 1 means it is less volatile.

Troubleshooting

Why are some charts empty?

This usually happens if we cannot fetch data for a specific ticker symbol. Check your CSV for typos (e.g., "APPL" vs "AAPL").

My CSV upload failed. What do I do?

Ensure your file is a valid .csv (not Excel .xlsx) and matches the schema shown in "Quick Links". Try reducing the file size if it's very large.

Found a bug or have a feature request?

We'd love to hear from you! Please contact our support team via the link in the footer.
