Platform Features

Comprehensive tools for professional portfolio analysis

Portfolio Management

Experience a unified view of your investments. Our platform supports diverse file formats including CSV, Excel, and JSON for seamless data import, giving you a complete picture of your financial health.

  • Multi-format data ingestion
  • Unified dashboard view
  • Automated asset categorization
  • Real-time value tracking

Risk Analytics

Navigate market volatility with confidence. Our advanced risk engine calculates Value at Risk (VaR) and Conditional VaR (CVaR) using sophisticated Monte Carlo simulations to stress-test your portfolio.

  • Monte Carlo simulations
  • Value at Risk (VaR) calculation
  • Drawdown analysis
  • Sharpe & Sortino ratios

Options Analysis

Unlock the power of derivatives with our professional grade tools. Analyze complex option strategies with real-time Greek calculations (Delta, Gamma, Theta, Vega) and visual payoff diagrams.

  • Real-time Greeks calculation
  • Covered call strategy finder
  • Volatility skew analysis
  • Payoff visualization

Brokerage Integration

Connect seamlessly to your brokerage accounts. Leveraging Plaid and SnapTrade integrations, we provide secure, real-time synchronization of your balances, positions, and transaction history.

  • Secure Plaid & SnapTrade connection
  • Automated daily sync
  • Bank-level encryption security
  • Support for major brokerages

Transaction Analysis

Deep dive into your trading history. Our granular transaction analysis module automatically categorizes trades, tracks wash sales, and provides detailed P&L attribution.

  • FIFO cost basis accounting
  • Wash sale tracking
  • Realized vs Unrealized P&L
  • Tax-efficiency reports

Performance Tracking

Monitor your success in real-time. Compare your returns against major benchmarks like SPY and VT. Our dynamic dashboards provide instant visibility into your alpha generation.

  • Benchmark comparison (SPY, VT)
  • Alpha & Beta calculation
  • Time-weighted returns
  • Daily & Monthly performance heatmaps
