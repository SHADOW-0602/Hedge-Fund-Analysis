High-Frequency Data Pipeline

Decision making requires up-to-the-second data. Our architecture ingests market data from multiple providers including Polygon, Finnhub, and Yahoo Finance, processing thousands of data points per second to give you a live view of the market.

  • Multi-provider redundancy for 99.9% uptime

  • Bank-level AES-256 encryption
  • Zero-knowledge proof authentication
  • SOC2 compliant infrastructure partners
  • Regular third-party security audits
